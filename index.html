Fractal Drift

Infinite 3D fractals that dance to your music.

PS5 controller: connect by USB or Bluetooth, then press any button. Esc/Options opens settings, G fullscreen.

Controls

PS5 DualSense

Left stickUp/down is the throttle (centred you drift on gently, back brakes). Left/right rolls – a fractal has no up, so the horizon stays where you put it
Right stickSteer - you go where you point. The craft leans into the turn and the horizon rolls back level on its own
R2 / L2Boost / brake (analog). Speed also scales with distance to the surface, so you can dive forever
L1 / R1Previous / next tab: Fractals · Tunnel · Canyon · Nebula · Songform
D-pad ← →Previous / next fractal
D-pad ↑ ↓Morph the fractal's shape
✕ CrossAutopilot on/off (sticks still steer)
□ SquareCycle colour palette
△ TriangleHide/show HUD
◯ CircleReset view
Touchpad clickCycle audio reactivity
RumbleThe controller rumbles with the bass. Strength is in Settings (changing it buzzes so you can feel the level). For Bluetooth, click Settings › Direct haptics once
L3Photo mode (max detail; hold still to refine)
R3Level the horizon
CreateScreenshot (saved to Downloads)
OptionsSettings menu
